## 👥 Who Can Join?

**Age requirement: 14–18 years old.**

We welcome young people who:
- Are passionate about technology.
- Enjoy learning and solving problems.
- Want to collaborate with an international team.

**Beginners are welcome!** You don't need to be an expert. Curiosity and commitment matter most.
